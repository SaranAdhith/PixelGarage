Briefing

Good To Know
Before We Start.

The things founders usually ask us first — answered upfront.

What is PixelGarage?

PixelGarage (also known as Pixel Garage) is a software development studio based in India that designs, builds, and launches custom iOS & Android apps, websites, backend systems, and AI automation workflows. Founded by Saran Adhith in 2024, the studio handles the complete software development lifecycle — from first idea to production — with a team of 8 engineers and 25+ QA testers.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We specialize in custom software development including: iOS & Android mobile app development, custom web development and website development, backend engineering and API development, AI and workflow automation, SaaS and MVP development for startups, and full product development from design to launch. We take on single-component projects or complete end-to-end product builds.

Do you build from scratch or use templates?

Everything is designed and coded custom from scratch to fit your brand and needs — no off-the-shelf templates. Every app, website, and backend system is engineered specifically for your requirements.

Who owns the code and product?

You do. We hand over 100% full ownership of the source code, backend infrastructure, and deployment accounts at launch. There is no vendor lock-in.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes — we stay on for bug fixes, performance updates, feature iterations, and security maintenance to keep your product healthy as it grows.

How long does a typical software development project take?

Most custom app development and web development projects ship in 4–12 weeks depending on scope and complexity. After scoping, we share a clear roadmap with milestones so you always know what's coming next.

How much does custom software development cost?

Pricing depends on scope and complexity. We quote a fixed price upfront with clear payment milestones and zero hidden fees — you'll know the full cost before we start building. Our pricing is competitive for India-based software development while maintaining high quality standards.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Absolutely. We can build alongside your in-house team, pick up an existing codebase, or take a project the full way on our own — whatever fits your setup.

What technology stack does PixelGarage use?

We use modern, proven technologies: Swift and SwiftUI for iOS, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android, React Native for cross-platform, React and Next.js for web, Node.js and Express for backends, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis for databases, AWS and Docker for cloud infrastructure, and n8n for AI automation workflows.

How does PixelGarage ensure software quality?

Every release is tested by 25+ QA testers before production deployment. Our quality assurance process includes manual testing, automated testing with Appium, penetration testing using Burp Suite, and adherence to OWASP security standards.

Do you build AI automation and workflow automation systems?

Yes. We build custom AI automation and workflow automation systems using self-hosted n8n. We've deployed 100+ AI automation workflows integrating Claude, OpenRouter LLMs, HubSpot CRM, Slack, and Google Sheets for clients like CommunityTracker.ai.

Is PixelGarage a good choice for startup MVP development?

Yes. We're ideal for startup MVP development — we offer fixed-cost pricing, fast delivery (4-12 weeks), full code ownership, and a founder-led team that understands startup constraints. You get a production-ready product, not just a prototype.

Where is PixelGarage located?

PixelGarage is based in India and serves clients worldwide. We work remotely with clients across India, the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.

Who founded PixelGarage?

PixelGarage was founded in 2024 by Saran Adhith (Founder & Software Engineer), along with co-founders Daraneesh (CTO) and Alwin N Joseph (Lead Developer). The studio is founder-led — the people who scope your project are the same ones who build and launch it.

How do we get started?

Tell us about your idea using the form below or email us directly at pixelgarage.info@gmail.com. We'll reply within 24 hours, scope the work, and share clear milestones and a fixed-cost quote.
