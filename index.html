01Understand ↗

Get to the real problem.

We talk through your users, constraints and goals. Then agree on the scope, milestones and a fixed cost before we write code.

Output / A clear brief & a shared plan
02Design ↗

Make it tangible.

Flows become screens. Screens become a prototype you can use. We resolve the awkward bits while they’re still easy to change.

Output / A product you can click through

We build in milestones, show you progress and test along the way. You stay close to the work and the people doing it.

Output / Tested, working software
